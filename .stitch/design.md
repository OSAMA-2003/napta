## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-density, authoritative visual language for enterprise agricultural telemetry, global trade, and precision agronomy. Merging the restrained discipline of developer tools with the organic depth of biosciences, the system delivers an executive SaaS experience tuned for planetary-scale observation, yield modeling, and commercial settlement.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Sovereign & Analytical:** Commands authority through precise spatial rhythms, ultra-crisp tabular layouts, and data density that honors complex scientific and economic realities without visual clutter.
- **Organic High-Tech:** Bridges raw earth science and machine intelligence using deep botanical emeralds, obsidian grounding surfaces, and bio-luminescent sensor accents.
- **Institutional Trust:** Evokes the operational confidence of tier-one financial infrastructure combined with sovereign satellite surveillance suites.

### Design Movement: Engineered Bio-Minimalism
The aesthetic synthesizes:
- **Linear-Grade Precision:** 1px structured ghost borders, monospaced numeric readouts, micro-badges, and instantaneous interaction states.
- **Glass-Infused Elevation:** Muted sub-surface translucency (`backdrop-blur-md`) with ultra-fine border highlights that separate analytical cards from the data canvas.
- **Telemetry Visualizations:** High-contrast radial gauges, sensor status dots with calibrated pulse states, and multi-layered metric sparklines.

## Colors

The palette operates on a calibrated dual-strata system: a pristine executive light canvas for daytime analytics, procurement, and contract execution, accented with obsidian telemetry panels for real-time remote sensing, satellite spectral bands, and hardware feeds.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#054F31`):** Deep Botanical Emerald. Grounded, executive, used for dominant interactive triggers, primary branding anchors, and key telemetry containers.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`):** Obsidian Dark Slate. Forms the bedrock of dark-mode surfaces, telemetry readouts, table headers, and structural chrome.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Bio-Luminescent Mint. Signifies positive yield trends, verified certifications, satellite sensor health, and primary interactive highlights.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Cool Slate Neutral. Systematically stepping from canvas base (`#F8FAFC`), through border structural lines (`#E2E8F0` / dark `#1E293B`), to muted metadata labels (`#64748B`).

### Functional Accents
- **Bio-Indicator Lime (`#84CC16`):** Vegetative vitality, optimal photosynthetic performance, and hyper-active sensor clusters.
- **Risk Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Moisture stress alerts, market volatility triggers, and agronomic threshold warnings.
- **Critical Crimson (`#EF4444`):** Pathogen outbreaks, extreme weather events, contract defaults.
- **Atmospheric Azure (`#0284C7`):** Irrigation telemetry, water rights allocations, and meteorological overlays.
